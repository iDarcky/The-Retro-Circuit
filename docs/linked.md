## Post 3: The "How I Built It" (Use on Thursday/Friday)
**Goal:** Share the technical/product journey. This appeals to your professional network and drives secondary traffic.
**Media:** A screenshot of your `docs/ROADMAP.md` file, a messy architecture diagram, or a picture of your code editor.

Shipping **The Retro Circuit** this week taught me a massive lesson about Product Management: Scope creep is terrifying when *you* are the sole developer. 😅

When navigating the build from November to today, I had to be ruthless with my MVP. I currently have a backlog of "shit tons of ideas", but I had to draw a line in the sand for this soft launch.

Here is the stack I used to pull this off as a non-technical PM:
⚡ **Next.js (App Router):** For the frontend architecture and fast routing.
🗄️ **Supabase:** For the database to house the 64 consoles and 133 variants.
🎨 **Tailwind CSS:** To bring the "Swiss Design" aesthetic to life without fighting custom CSS.
🤖 **AI Assistants:** My absolute secret weapon for debugging, pair-programming, and unblocking myself when I hit a wall.

To all the PMs, designers, and non-tech folks out there—the barrier to building software has never been lower. If you have an idea, start building it.
